PRIVACY POLICY

Profe is a training and nutrition app made in Paraguay. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. Questions: formapy.fitness@gmail.com.

1. What we collect

  • Your account: when you sign in with Apple, we receive your email address (or Apple's private relay address) and the first name you choose to share.
  • Your profile and plan: your goal, the days and minutes you train, your equipment, experience, any limits you tell us about, and your membership status.
  • What you log: workouts and sets, cardio, meals you photograph or describe, your weight, your weekly check-ins, and the questions you ask Profe.
  • Apple Health, only if you allow it: we read sleep, steps, weight, active calories and your Move goal, and cycle tracking only if you turn on "Plan around my cycle". We save the workouts you finish to Apple Health, with their duration, calories and distance.

We don't use advertising or analytics SDKs, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

2. Photos

  • Meal photos are sent to our AI provider only to read the meal, and are not stored on our servers.
  • Espejo photos and week photos stay on your phone. They are never uploaded and never sent to AI.

3. Where it is stored

A copy of your account and logs is stored with our hosting provider, Supabase, so your data comes back if you change phones. Each account can only reach its own data. Your data may be processed outside your country.

4. How we use it

Only to provide Profe: build and adjust your plan, estimate your meals, show your progress and answer your questions. We never sell your data and never use it for ads. Apple Health data is never used for advertising or marketing and is never stored in iCloud by us.

5. Artificial intelligence

Meal photo reading and Ask Profe use Anthropic's Claude API as our provider. These features are off until you agree to them, and you can turn them off in Settings, AI analysis. Profe sends only what is needed to answer: the meal photo or description, or your question with the recent data it refers to. Espejo and week photos are never sent.

6. Payments

On iPhone, Apple processes payments; on the web, Stripe does. We never see or store your card details.

7. How long we keep it

While your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete your data from our servers. Copies in our provider's routine backups are removed when those backups expire.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account in the app: Settings, Your data, Delete my account.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data or for deletion by email.
  • Turn off Apple Health access in the Health app at any time, and turn off AI features in Settings.

9. Children

Profe is for adults. People under 18 are guided to a log-only mode without automatic targets, and we don't knowingly collect data from children under 13.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date above, and tell you in the app for important changes.
