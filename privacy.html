PRIVACY POLICY

This outline reflects how Profe works today. Counsel must complete and review it before launch. The Terms of Use follow the same structure.

1. What we collect

Your account (Sign in with Apple), profile and goal; workouts; meals; weight; weekly check-ins; the questions you ask Profe; sleep and steps from Apple Health if you allow it. Photos stay on your phone: a meal photo is sent only to be read and is not stored, and Espejo and week photos never leave your phone.

2. Where it is stored

A copy of your logs is stored with our hosting provider, Supabase, so your data comes back if you change phones. Each account can only reach its own data.

3. How we use it

Only to provide the service: build and adjust your plan, estimate your meals and answer your questions. Never for ads or sold.

4. Apple Health

We read sleep, steps and weight, and save the workouts you finish. We never store health data in iCloud.

5. Artificial intelligence

Meal estimates and Ask Profe use Anthropic’s Claude API as our provider. Espejo and week photos are never sent.

6. Payments

On iPhone, Apple processes payments. On the web, Stripe does. We never store your card details.

7. How long we keep it

While your account is open. Deleting your account deletes your data from our servers. [Backup periods to be confirmed by counsel]

8. Your rights and deleting your account

In the app: Settings, Your data, Delete my account. You can also ask us for a copy or for deletion by email.
